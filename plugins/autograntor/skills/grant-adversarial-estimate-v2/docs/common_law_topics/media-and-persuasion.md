# Common Law topic file: media-and-persuasion

> **Packaging note (prototype, not part of the Common Law).** Media, audiences, creators, games and campaigns. Read with `PERCYs_v3.7_compact.md`. Ruling text is copied unchanged from the published Common Law (version 2026-10-18.2). Which ruling goes in which topic file is the packager's guess.

### CL-24: Games as an exposure class, and their borrowed streaming reach [Proposed 2026-10-17, by Claude]

**Rule:** For a video game, split owners by playtime: heavy players (>20h) as Sustained (Y=3) with Δ cut by a message-fidelity factor (≈0.4–0.6 when the argument is carried by mechanics rather than text); 1–20h as Long-form; <1h and free-demo players as Glance; never-launched owners at zero. Stream audiences are borrowed reach counted in unique viewers: full let's-play viewers ≈0.3 per copy sold (Engaged, Long-form), clip/feed viewers ≈2.5 per copy (General, Glance). Apply the fidelity cut once, to Δ; don't also lower Y or add a separate blanket backfire haircut. **Why:** The PERCYs exposure classes don't describe deep interactive play; without a rule, estimators either treated all players as a year-long returning audience or double-discounted them.

### CL-25: Cultural-hit lump for mass media [Proposed 2026-10-17, by Claude]

**Rule:** For a game, film or similar product, add at most one step row for a cultural breakout, only at clear thresholds (e.g. ≥300k and ≥1M owners for a game; nothing at 100k), priced at no more than about 1/8 of the smallest comparable listed social-shift entry, counted once; the audience below those thresholds stays in the per-person PERCYs rows. **Why:** CL-6 makes listed entries (IABIED bestseller, AI 2027) upper bounds and CL-5 forbids re-counting the same audience; the nearest precedent (Universal Paperclips) produced no listed milestone.

### CL-26: Talent redirected by entertainment media [Proposed 2026-10-17, by Claude]

**Rule:** Price people a piece of media moves into safety work as discrete (Poisson) events. Anchor the rate on HPMOR (~1 per 5k readers) scaled down by how explicitly the work points at the field (≈1/20–1/30 for a work that never names it), with "would have entered anyway" inside the rate. Value each at pre-2030 researcher-years (with a ramp) plus post-2030 years at the TAI discount, at the researcher-year price. **Why:** The PERCYs pipeline rule prices communicator output, not research careers, and this row is often the largest one in a media grant, so it needs a common anchor.

### CL-30: Applicant-defined reach metrics need an audit before conversion [Proposed 2026-10-17, by Claude]

**Rule:** Before converting an applicant's topical reach figure (e.g. "topic-relevant views") to PERCYs, check any single item over ~25% of the total and look for an independent audit. Convert only strictly on-topic units at the full PERCYs rate and the applicant's broader category at about one-third of it; adjacent content that does not move the AI-risk scale counts at ≤0.25. If no audit exists, assume the applicant's figure is about 3× the strict one. Apply any salience-saturation factor after major warning-shot events to general-audience Δ only, and state it. **Why:** A single viral adjacent item can dominate an applicant's reported total, and independent audits can find the strictly on-topic figure is a small fraction of the headline; Δ per glance-view also falls once most of the audience has a handle on the issue.
